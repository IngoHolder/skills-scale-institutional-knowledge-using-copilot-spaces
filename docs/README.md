# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

This folder contains the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme across the lifecycle of cross-functional product work. The process emphasizes clear ownership, measurable outcomes, iterative delivery, and strong communication so teams can move from idea to release with alignment and minimal risk.

OctoAcme begins by validating the business case and stakeholder need through project initiation. A project one-pager defines the problem, goals, success metrics, timelines, and initial risks so a go/no-go decision can be made before planning begins. Once approved, the team moves into planning, where the work is broken into a prioritized backlog, acceptance criteria are defined, dependencies are identified, and a release plan and Definition of Done are established to keep delivery realistic and testable.

During execution, the team operates with regular standups, sprint or milestone reviews, and a shared project board to track work through backlog, in progress, review, QA, and done. Product, project, and engineering roles work together through clear responsibilities: the Product Manager sets priorities and outcomes, the Project Manager coordinates schedules and communication, and developers and QA validate quality and delivery. Risk management is ongoing: issues, blockers, and dependencies are tracked in a risk register, weekly syncs review status, and escalations follow a defined path from team to sponsor when needed.

Before release, OctoAcme requires passing tests and security checks, release notes, rollback plans, and smoke-test verification. After every release, milestone, or incident, teams hold a retrospective to capture what went well, what could improve, and which action items need owners and deadlines. This cycle of planning, execution, review, and continuous improvement allows OctoAcme to deliver customer value while maintaining quality, transparency, and operational discipline.
