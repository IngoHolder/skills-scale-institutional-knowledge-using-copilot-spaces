# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines the typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. It is intended to make ownership, collaboration, and decision boundaries clearer across the full project lifecycle.

> Role assignments may be combined for smaller teams or adjusted to fit a specific project context. The goal is to clarify expectations without implying every project needs every role.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- Work closely with Product Managers to confirm priority and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with Project Managers on delivery timing, dependencies, and blockers
- Partner with Technical Leads or Architects on implementation standards and technical trade-offs
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to validate quality and defect resolution

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Project Managers to balance scope, timelines, and dependencies
- Work with UX/UI Designers or Researchers to validate user needs and usability
- Coordinate with Technical Leads on feasibility, constraints, and technical trade-offs
- Align with QA/Testing and Operations on release readiness and business impact

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- Align with Product Managers on priorities, milestones, and trade-offs
- Work with Technical Leads and Developers on sequencing, blocker escalation, and delivery health
- Coordinate release communication with Operations/SRE or Release Managers and stakeholders
- Support Scrum Masters or Delivery Coaches in facilitation and team improvement efforts

---

## UX/UI Designers or User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers help teams understand user needs, refine workflows, and ensure that solutions are useful, accessible, and easy to adopt.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and interpret user feedback and research findings
- Translate user needs into journeys, wireframes, and design requirements
- Validate designs with usability testing and iteration
- Partner with Product Managers on discovery and prioritization
- Support Developers in turning design intent into feasible implementation

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework caused by unclear requirements or weak user validation
- Ensure product decisions are grounded in user outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Discovery sessions and design review meetings
- Prototype walkthroughs and usability feedback loops
- Cross-functional planning and acceptance criteria refinement

### Key Interactions
- Work with Product Managers to shape desired user outcomes and success metrics
- Collaborate with Developers and Technical Leads to ensure implementation aligns with design intent
- Partner with QA/Testing to validate usability and regression risk in critical flows
- Share research findings with Project Managers so change impacts and dependencies are understood

---

## Technical Leads or Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical direction, engineering standards, and design quality. They help ensure the team makes sound implementation decisions while balancing product needs and delivery constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture, technical standards, and design patterns
- Review design choices and implementation trade-offs
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and long-term maintainability concerns
- Support estimation and sequencing for complex work
- Help coordinate technical decisions across teams or services

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable technical solutions
- Reduce avoidable rework and architectural drift
- Support predictable delivery with clear technical guardrails

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records and technical design reviews
- Dependency and risk discussions with engineering leads and PMs
- Technical planning updates during roadmap and release review

### Key Interactions
- Work with Developers to guide implementation and code quality standards
- Partner with Product Managers to assess feasibility, impact, and trade-offs
- Coordinate with Project Managers on capacity, sequencing, and risk escalations
- Support QA/Testing in identifying test coverage gaps and operational concerns

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs into clear business and process definitions. They help ensure requirements are understandable, testable, and aligned with the intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify goals, process flows, and stakeholder requirements
- Map current and desired business processes
- Turn requirements into testable acceptance criteria
- Support backlog refinement and prioritization discussions
- Identify gaps, dependencies, or operational impacts early

### Goals
- Improve clarity and consistency across business and delivery teams
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and acceptance testing
- Support better decisions with measurable business context

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and backlog refinement sessions
- Process mapping and stakeholder review meetings
- Cross-functional handoff discussions with QA and delivery teams

### Key Interactions
- Work with Product Managers to refine problem statements and success criteria
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to ensure requirements are actionable and testable
- Share process changes or dependency impacts with Project Managers and stakeholders
- Support Technical Leads when requirements reveal architecture or workflow constraints

---

## Scrum Masters or Delivery Coaches

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Coaches help the team work effectively, remove impediments, and improve how the team delivers value. They focus on team health, process flow, and continuous improvement rather than product direction.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help identify and remove delivery impediments
- Support clear roles, rituals, and team collaboration norms
- Encourage continuous improvement through feedback and retrospectives
- Help maintain healthy planning and execution practices

### Goals
- Improve team efficiency, flow, and transparency
- Make delivery smoother and more predictable
- Support a learning culture and healthy collaboration patterns

### Typical Communication
- Team facilitation and issue tracking discussions
- Retrospective notes and action items
- Cross-team alignment on process blockers and improvement opportunities

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Project Managers on planning cadence, dependencies, and delivery risk
- Support Product Managers by keeping the team aligned on priorities and outcomes
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to reduce friction in delivery and decision-making
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to ensure quality checks are built into the work flow

---

## Operations / SRE / Release Managers

### Role Summary
Operations, SRE, and Release Managers help ensure services are reliable, observable, and ready for deployment. They focus on operational readiness, release coordination, and production health.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on availability, reliability, monitoring, and alerting needs
- Review deployment readiness and rollback plans
- Coordinate release windows, validation, and communication
- Support incident response and production triage when needed
- Help maintain operational quality and recovery readiness

### Goals
- Reduce production risk and improve service reliability
- Make releases predictable and observable
- Improve operational readiness and response time during incidents

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and deployment readiness reviews
- Incident updates and operational status communication
- Monitoring and reliability dashboards with stakeholders and delivery teams

### Key Interactions
- Collaborate with Project Managers on release planning and communications
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to validate operational readiness and smoke tests
- Partner with Product Managers and Stakeholders on release impact and support expectations
- Coordinate with Technical Leads on architecture, monitoring, and resilience requirements

---

## QA / Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA and Testing leads validate product quality, identify defects early, and help ensure releases meet acceptance criteria and user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define the QA strategy and test approach for a release or milestone
- Validate functionality against acceptance criteria and requirements
- Identify defects, regressions, and risk areas before launch
- Collaborate with developers on problem resolution and verification
- Support release readiness assessments and smoke testing

### Goals
- Reduce quality risk before release
- Improve product confidence and user trust
- Ensure changes meet agreed standards and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, issue triage, and defect status updates
- QA checkpoints during planning, execution, and release review
- Release readiness sign-off discussions with PMs and stakeholders

### Key Interactions
- Work with Developers on issue reproduction and validation
- Partner with Business Analysts and Product Managers on acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Coordinate with Operations/SRE or Release Managers on launch readiness and release checks
- Support Project Managers in identifying quality blockers and risk impact

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders provide business context, sponsorship, and decision-making input. They help set direction, approve outcomes, and ensure the work aligns with broader business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, constraints, and success criteria
- Review progress, trade-offs, and major decisions
- Provide sponsorship, approvals, and prioritization guidance
- Share business context relevant to roadmap and release timing

### Goals
- Ensure investments support strategic priorities and customer value
- Maintain visibility into delivery progress and risk
- Support informed decisions and timely approvals

### Typical Communication
- Status reviews, roadmap updates, and milestone reviews
- Business approvals and sponsor escalations when needed
- Release updates and outcome assessments

### Key Interactions
- Work with Product Managers and Project Managers to align on priorities and trade-offs
- Provide input to UX/UI teams, Business Analysts, and technical leads on goals and constraints
- Collaborate with Operations/SRE or Release Managers on release impact and readiness
- Support QA/Testing and Developers by clarifying expected outcomes and acceptance standards

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles should be adapted to fit the team size and project context; not every project needs every persona in a fully separate capacity.
